# EDB

> EnterpriseDB (EDB) is a database software and services company that develops EDB Postgres AI, a Postgres-based platform for transactional, analytical, and agentic AI workloads, deployable on-premises, in the cloud, or hybrid. EDB has contributed to Postgres core for 20 years and reports over 1,700 enterprise customers.

EDB Postgres AI unifies transactional, analytical, and agentic AI workloads on a single Postgres-based platform, deployable across on-premises, cloud, and hybrid environments under one control plane. EDB was named a Leader in The Forrester Wave™: Multimodel Data Platforms, Q2 2026, with the highest score in the strategy category of all 14 evaluated vendors.

Product documentation lives at a separate index: [https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/llms.txt](https://www.enterprisedb.com/docs/llms.txt).
