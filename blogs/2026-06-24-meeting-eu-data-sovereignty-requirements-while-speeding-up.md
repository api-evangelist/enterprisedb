Data sovereignty has moved from a background concern to a board-level priority across Europe. In June 2026, the European Commission set out its European Tech Sovereignty package, signaling a clear policy direction: organizations, and the public sector in particular, are being encouraged to keep greater control over where their data lives and who can access it. For many organizations, this has turned a long-standing question into an immediate one.
