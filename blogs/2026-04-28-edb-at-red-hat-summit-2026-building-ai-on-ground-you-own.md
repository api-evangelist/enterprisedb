EDB is heading to Red Hat Summit 2026 in Atlanta (May 11–14) to show what sovereign, production-grade AI looks like when it's built on infrastructure you actually own. The EDB and Red Hat collaboration centers on EDB Postgres AI on Red Hat OpenShift, a unified data layer that handles both structured data and vector embeddings in a single Postgres instance, eliminating the need for separate vector stores or proprietary pipelines.
