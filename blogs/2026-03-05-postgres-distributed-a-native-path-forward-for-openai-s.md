Scaling a single-primary PostgreSQL database to hundreds of millions of users requires heroic manual interventions and creates mounting operational debt. Discover how transitioning to a native, active-active architecture with EDB Postgres Distributed (PGD) eliminates the "Write Wall" and polyglot fragmentation. Learn how to retire complex workarounds and deploy a truly resilient, self-healing data platform.
